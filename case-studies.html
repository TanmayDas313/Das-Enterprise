Retail & Supermarket Client Case A

Multi-Store Consumer Goods Retail Chain

The Challenge:

Managing four retail locations in Gujarat with disjointed offline software led to severe inventory discrepancies, frequent cashier reconciliation variances, and delayed consolidated GSTR-1 filings.

The Hisab Kitab Solution:

Deployed Tally Prime Multi-User Gold on dedicated Cloud servers. Configured barcode scanner POS billing with automated UPI QR displays, central master pricing control, and scheduled nightly data sync.

Quantitative Results & ROI

68% Faster Billing Average checkout time per customer dropped from 90 seconds to under 28 seconds.
0.0% Stock Variance Eliminated untracked shrinkage through automated closing register stock validation.
100% On-Time GSTR-1 Centralized GST return ready on 2nd of every month without spreadsheet collation.
Manufacturing Client Case B

Industrial Valves & Precision Machining Unit

The Challenge:

The client used manual Excel workbooks for tracking component consumption across lathe and milling operations. Scrap rates were unrecorded, leading to inaccurate unit cost pricing and compromised gross profit margins.

The Hisab Kitab Solution:

Constructed multi-level Bill of Materials (BOM) in Tally Prime. Created job-work inward/outward tracking registers, automated scrap byproduct credit, and real-time machine hour expense allocation.

Quantitative Results & ROI

4.2% Margin Uplift Identified and corrected under-costed assemblies during quotation phase.
22 Hours Saved / Month Accountants eliminated manual reconciliation of scrap and job work challans.
Zero Missing e-Way Bills Every factory dispatch automatically synchronizes with NIC portal for instantaneous IRN.
Wholesale & Distribution Client Case C

State-Wide Electrical Goods Distributor

The Challenge:

Supplying 350+ dealers with complex trade discounts and varying credit terms caused high days sales outstanding (DSO) and frequent payment disputes regarding interest and scheme credits.

The Hisab Kitab Solution:

Engineered custom price lists inside Tally Prime linked to dealer volume tiers. Configured automated overdue reminder statements with PDF invoice attachments dispatched via WhatsApp.

Quantitative Results & ROI

35% Reduction in DSO Average payment recovery cycle accelerated from 62 days down to 40 days.
₹18.5L Working Capital Freed Significantly reduced interest costs on the company's bank cash credit (CC) facility.
