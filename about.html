Our Story & Heritage

Empowering Indian Business With Financial Clarity

Hisab Kitab, powered by Das Enterprises, was established with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between traditional Indian commercial bookkeeping and modern, technology-driven business intelligence.

The Partnership

Hisab Kitab + Das Enterprises

For over a decade, Das Enterprises has functioned as a trusted technology and business consulting partner for trading, manufacturing, and service establishments across Western India.

Recognizing that entrepreneurs often struggle with fragmented ledgers, cumbersome inventory counts, and stressful month-end GST deadlines, we created Hisab Kitab: an integrated business management platform that leverages the rock-solid reliability of Tally Prime, elevated by bespoke customization, cloud synchronization, and dedicated human support.

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all software. We take the time to understand your chart of accounts, godown locations, billing volume, and reporting requirements to configure a solution that works for you from day one.

Our Core Principles

1. Technological Precision Zero tolerance for rounding discrepancies, mismatched tax registers, or corrupt data tables.
2. Rapid Human Assistance When an accountant encounters an urgent billing error, they talk to an experienced specialist within minutes, not a chatbot.
3. Absolute Data Confidentiality Your financial margins and customer records remain strictly under your control with enterprise encryption.
