Pillar 01

Accounting & GST Compliance

GST rules are dynamic and compliance mistakes are costly. Hisab Kitab configures Tally Prime to automatically compute CGST, SGST, and IGST based on customer state codes, validate HSN codes, and generate one-click returns.

✓
Automated GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B: Error prevention mechanisms highlight missing GSTINs or mismatched tax rates before filing.
✓
Direct e-Invoicing (IRN) & e-Way Bill: Generate digitally signed QR codes directly from the invoicing screen without logging into the NIC portal.
✓
GSTR-2B ITC Matching: Reconcile your purchase ledgers against supplier filings on the GST portal to ensure 100% legitimate input tax credit claim.
✓
Auto Bank Reconciliation (e-Banking): Import statements from SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and instantly match cheques and NEFT transfers.

Compliance Safeguards Summary

Zero-Error Invoicing: Blocks billing when HSN length or GST percentage does not conform to statutory tariffs.
Automated Debit/Credit Notes: Seamless linkage with original invoice numbers and reason codes.
TDS & TCS Integrated: Automatic tax deduction at appropriate slab rates with 26Q and 27EQ generation.
